# SilentWitness India
Free Legal Rights AI for 700M+ Underserved Indians — Powered by Gemma 4

## Problem
700 million Indians face legal situations with zero access to legal counsel.

## Solution  
SilentWitness uses Gemma 4 (gemma-4-e4b-it) to provide instant legal guidance in Hindi + English, free, with document understanding.

## Features
- Legal rights in Hindi + English
- Covers: Tenant rights, Labor law, Consumer protection, Domestic violence, Criminal rights
- Evidence package generator with timestamp
