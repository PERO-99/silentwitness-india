# ⚖️ SilentWitness India
### Free Legal Rights AI for 700M+ Underserved Indians — Powered by Gemma 4

## The Problem
700 million Indians face legal situations every year with zero access to legal counsel.
A landlord illegally evicts someone. A factory owner violates labor laws. A domestic
violence victim doesn't know what counts as evidence. They can't afford lawyers.

## The Solution
SilentWitness uses **Gemma 4 (gemma-4-e4b-it)** to provide instant legal guidance in
**Hindi + English**, completely free, with document understanding via multimodal vision.

## Features
- Legal rights explanation in Hindi + English
- Document/contract photo analysis (Gemma 4 multimodal)
- Evidence package generator with timestamp
- Covers: Tenant rights, Labor law, Consumer protection, Domestic violence, Criminal rights

## Laws Covered
- Indian Contract Act 1872
- Rent Control Acts (State-wise)
- Minimum Wages Act
- POSH Act 2013
- Consumer Protection Act 2019
- IPC / CrPC
- Domestic Violence Act 2005

## Tech Stack
- **Model:** Google Gemma 4 (gemma-4-e4b-it) via HuggingFace Transformers
- **UI:** Gradio
- **Platform:** Kaggle (T4 GPU)
